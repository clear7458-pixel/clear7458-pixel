I’m naturally quiet and shy, especially around people I don’t know well. I may seem distant or awkward at first, but it doesn’t mean I dislike you.

I spend a lot of time listening to music, playing games, exploring my interests, or simply enjoying my own space. Because of that, I may reply late, disappear for a while, or become quiet without meaning anything negative.

I’m also a Highly Sensitive Person (HSP), so I tend to notice small details, pick up on people’s emotions, and sometimes feel things more deeply than others might expect.

「 𝚆𝚑𝚎𝚗 𝙸 𝚐𝚘 𝚚𝚞𝚒𝚎𝚝 」

Please don’t immediately assume that my silence means I’m angry, ignoring you, or that I dislike you.

Sometimes I’m tired, overwhelmed, lost in my thoughts, or simply don’t know what to say.

My silence can sometimes be misunderstood unintentionally. Someone may assume what I meant, and a small misunderstanding can turn into an argument. What makes it exhausting is having to explain or fix a situation that I never intended to cause.

If something feels wrong, please ask me directly instead of assuming. I’d rather clear things up calmly than let a misunderstanding become something bigger.

My social battery can be unpredictable. Some days I can talk a lot, while other days I need quiet time to recharge.

Being quiet doesn’t mean I don’t care.

I can enjoy talking to someone and still need space. I can care about people while also enjoying my own company.

Once I become comfortable and trust someone, I can be much more open, talkative, playful, and affectionate.
