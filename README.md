## 「 𝚆𝚑𝚎𝚗 𝙸 𝚐𝚘 𝚚𝚞𝚒𝚎𝚝 」

Please don’t immediately assume my silence means I’m angry, ignoring you, or that I dislike you.

Sometimes I’m tired, overwhelmed, lost in my thoughts, or simply don’t know what to say.

My silence can sometimes cause misunderstandings when people assume what I mean. Small misunderstandings can turn into arguments, and it can be exhausting when I have to explain or fix something I never intended to cause.

If something feels wrong, please ask me directly instead of assuming. I’d rather communicate calmly than let misunderstandings grow.
