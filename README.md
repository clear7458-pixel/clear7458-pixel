I’m naturally quiet and shy, especially around people I don’t know well. I may seem distant or awkward at first, but it doesn’t mean I dislike you. I simply need time to feel comfortable and build trust.

I spend a lot of my time listening to music, playing games, exploring my interests, or enjoying my own space. Because of that, I may reply late, disappear for a while, or become quiet without meaning anything negative by it.

I’m also a Highly Sensitive Person (HSP), so I tend to notice small details, emotions, words, and changes in tone more deeply than people might expect.

«「 𝚆𝚑𝚎𝚗 𝙸 𝚐𝚘 𝚚𝚞𝚒𝚎𝚝 」»

Please don’t assume that my silence means I’m angry, ignoring you, or that I dislike you.

Sometimes I’m simply tired, overwhelmed, lost in my thoughts, or unsure of what to say. My silence is usually about my own state of mind, not about the person I’m talking to.

Sometimes my quietness can unintentionally cause misunderstandings. People may assume what I meant or how I feel, and a small misunderstanding can grow into an argument.

What makes this especially exhaustiis is having to explain, clear up, or fix a situation that I never intended to cause.

If something feels wrong, please ask me directly before making assumptions. I would rather communicate calmly and clear things up than let an unnecessary misunderstanding become something bigger.

«「 𝙼𝚢 𝚜𝚘𝚌𝚒𝚊𝚕 𝚋𝚊𝚝𝚝𝚎𝚛𝚢 」»

My social battery can change a lot. Some days I can talk for hours; other days I need to disappear and recharge by myself.

Being quiet doesn’t mean I don’t care.

I can enjoy someone’s company and still need space. I can care about people while needing time alone. If I become comfortable and trust someone, I can become much more open, talkative, playful, and affectionate.

So please don’t take my quiet moments personally. Sometimes I’m simply recharging.

Silence isn’t always rejection.
Distance isn’t always dislike.
And needing space doesn’t mean I stopped caring.
